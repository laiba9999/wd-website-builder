Before you ask

Questions

What should I wear?

Each event has its own dress code listed above. If you're only coming to one, dress for that one.

Is there parking?

Yes at all three venues. Ferry Lane at Kew is free after 6pm; the Ealing car park charges until 8pm.

Can I bring my children?

Children are very welcome at the Mehndi and the Nikah. The Walima is adults only after 9pm.

When do you need my RSVP?

By 1 March 2027, so we can give the caterers a number they can work with.
